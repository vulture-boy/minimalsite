Tyson Moll - good mornings come easy
a eulogy for a discarded vaporizer
I spill out of the labour machine and flow along the floors, wood panels so you know the oils will really seep in

my eyeballs emerge from the brown sewage and gaze upon the green velvet sofa parallel to the crezenda that we will soon affix our gaze, my dear readers. an inverted splash and I am a thick glaze of mottled fibres and with a wave of my hands the lights come on. i am the king of thorns. I bring the lights. I am but crucified. but I am only a man with too much imagination and this media edifice before me will drain these thoughts of ego so that I may once again hold a clear jelatin form and rest within the fridge thise sundaye eveninge. the game begins. today, i will cross the hills and rivers in search of external validation for my labours. tomorrow, i will parody the mouse that has taken residence in my apartment and play the hollower. maybe I will enjoy an indie poem in play-forme. now I am emptied iwas/besomeone/elsebeing/former and beingsome/oneelse/ibe/before i am now/present

put me into my silico mold where i fit best and let me dream the sweetest dreams under a mask to block the creeeeeping of the light in the moring’s razor gaze and sweeten me with sugars and flavours so that I can be fun at parties

good mornings come easy
